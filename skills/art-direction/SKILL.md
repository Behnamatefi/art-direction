# Art direction

Read `art-direction.md` at the root of this repository before designing or reviewing an artifact. It is the source of truth for tokens, typography, layout, accessibility, language, theme, motion, and medium-specific guidance. If the skill was installed separately and the root file is unavailable, fetch it from `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Behnamatefi/art-direction/main/art-direction.md`.

1. Identify the requested medium and read its section in §10, including §10.4 for mobile and §10.5 for long proposals or reports when relevant.
2. Use the user's actual content and requirements. Apply the guide's English and Persian payloads and dark and light states, including the applicable controls and print behavior.
3. Review the finished artifact against §12. Check the actual output in the relevant states and sizes where possible. Report unresolved failures and any checks that could not be performed.

The user's explicit instructions take precedence when they conflict with the guide. Keep project-specific data and business terms supplied by the user separate from the reusable visual direction.
